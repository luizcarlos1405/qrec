# Design System: Qrec

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Torchlit Swamp Workbench"**

Qrec is a workbench in a swamp at night. It is sturdy, practical and tinted by the bog, and the only open flame is the REC torch. It is an **Operate** surface: someone who has used OBS or an NLE should find the canvas, the layer panel, the timeline and the record button where they expect them before they notice the swamp. The world lends four things only: the **palette** (every neutral is tinted from its theme's hue, never grey), the **type** (a hyperlegible UI sans, a tabular mono for every number that moves, and a storybook serif kept for a few display moments), the **density** (a pro-editor's 14 px UI with 32 px controls and a dense timeline), and **one signature move**.

The signature move is **the torchlit take**. You press `R` and a torch-red REC badge (an onion with ogre-ear trumpets) starts to flicker, a dashed ghost clip grows on the timeline, and live meters move. You press `R` again and the torch goes out: the ghost settles into a solid clip wearing its take color. Each of the 8 take colors (mossy green to fairy blue) then follows that take through every split, autocut fragment and bin row. The export bar fills with bubbling mud and ends with _"Layers! Like an onion. Your video is ready."_ Everything else is quiet, aligned and familiar.

**The scene that picks the default theme.** A developer sits at a desk in a dim room lit by two monitors and a ring light, recording a talk. Qrec sits on a side monitor, or under the windows being recorded. Between takes the user glances at it for a second or two to confirm the take landed. That is why **Swamp Night** is the default: low glare, and the canvas reads like a lit stage. **Swamp Day** is for daylight rooms. **Dragon's Keep** and **Ogre Mode** are personality themes on the same token names.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Four themes, one token set. Neutrals are tinted per theme, and three semantic hues each own exactly one job: green for what you act on, gold for time, red for recording and destruction.
- Take colors are data, shown on 4 px stripes, never decoration.
- Every number that changes (timecode, frame count, dB, ms, size) is JetBrains Mono, so digits never jitter.
- The key is always visible. Key-cap chips appear in the hint bar, the Record button, tooltips, menus, the palette and the cheat sheet.
- Surfaces are flat tonal layers with 1 px hairlines. Shadows exist only on things that float.
- Motion is functional (150–250 ms), with two authored moments: the torch flicker and the mud. Each has a reduced-motion fallback.
- Humor lives in secondary text, empty states and the icon. Labels are plain verbs.

**Theme mechanics.** pen.dev holds one variable per token with a `theme` axis (`swamp-night`, `swamp-day`, `dragons-keep`, `ogre-mode`). GTK gets one CSS file per theme that defines the same custom properties. In the frontmatter, Swamp Night tokens are unprefixed and the others carry `day-`, `keep-` and `ogre-` prefixes. The tables below are generated from the same source as the frontmatter, which is normative.

## Colors

The palette is restrained but committed. Bog-tinted neutrals fill the screen, and three single-job hues and eight data hues sit on top.

### Primary

- **Ogre Skin** (`accent`): the color of **acting on something**. It fills primary buttons, outlines the selected clip and row, draws the focus ring and the insertion caret, and turns switches and the slider fill on. It never decorates. In Dragon's Keep it is the pink "dragon scale".

### Secondary

- **Onion Gold** (`accent-2`): the color of **where you are in time and space**. It draws the playhead, the canvas snap guides and the timeline snap flash. It never carries text: as text it fails AA in Swamp Day, and P5 only lifts it to the 3:1 that indicators need.

### Tertiary

- **Torch** (`record`): the color of **recording and destruction**, and nothing else. It fills the REC badge, the Record/Stop button, the destructive button, the ghost clip outline and the meter's clip zone. Its text form is `record-text` (P6). Recording state is never shown by color alone: it always carries the ● onion glyph, the word "REC" and the flicker.

### Neutral

- **Bog at midnight** (`bg`): the window ground and the theater around the preview. Timeline lanes, input wells and the cheat-sheet backdrop use it too.
- **Wet moss** (`surface`): working chrome, meaning the header bar, Onion layers panel, timeline toolbar, track headers, ruler and hint bar.
- **Log** (`raised`): things that sit on top, meaning popovers, menus, toasts, modals, the palette, the clip body and secondary buttons.
- **Reeds** (`border`) and `border-strong`. Reeds are 1 px decorative separators. `border-strong` outlines interactive controls (≥ 3:1).
- **Parchment** (`text`) and **Lichen** (`muted`): primary and secondary text. `muted` also draws ruler labels and waveforms.
- **Swamp gas** (`warning`): silence hatch, warning icons, conflicts. Its text form is `warning-text`.
- **Outhouse door** (`wood`): the dark sediment band in the mud export bar and the material in illustrations (the signpost, the outhouse door). `mud` is wood lightened to read against `bg`.

### Take colors

Eight categorical colors per theme. They are tuned to stay distinguishable on that theme's `bg` and to keep away from `record` and `accent`. A take's color comes from its number: `take n → order[(n − 1) mod 8]`. The cycle order puts very different colors next to each other, so consecutive takes, which usually sit side by side on the timeline, always contrast. Under color-vision deficiency some pairs come close (for example mossy green and mud for deuteranopes in Swamp Day), so **take identity is never color-only**. The clip label shows the take number, and tooltips and bin rows name it.

### Usage rules (no PO decision needed)

- **R1 Selected rows.** A selected row gets the `selected` tint, a 1 px `accent` inner stroke (the stroke carries the state at ≥ 3:1), and **all of its text turns `text`**. `muted` and `accent-text` fail on the tint.
- **R2 Rows on `raised`** (menus, palette, dropdowns, popover lists) highlight on hover with the R1 treatment, not the `hover` overlay. Rows on `surface` use the `hover` overlay and keep `muted`.
- **R3 Text forms.** Any text or icon ≤ 20 px drawn on `bg`, `surface` or `raised` uses `accent-text`, `record-text` or `warning-text`, never the base color. Base colors are for fills, strokes, meters and large glyphs.
- **R4 `accent-2` never carries text.** Pair the playhead timecode flag as `text` on `raised` with a 1 px `playhead` stroke.
- **R5 Over video,** use `video-scrim` and `video-text` in every theme. Video content is theme-independent.
- **R6 Hover keeps contrast.** Flat buttons and links drawn in a text form keep it under the `hover` overlay; the decided values pass there too, so no separate hover color is needed.

### Named Rules

**The One Torch Rule.** `record` means "recording" or "this destroys something". Errors use `record-text` icons with plain `text` messages. No other element pulses, and nothing else is torch-red.

**The Single-Job Hue Rule.** Green (`accent`) is what you act on, gold (`accent-2`) is where you are in time, red (`record`) is recording and destruction. If an element needs color for any other reason, it uses a neutral.

**The Take Stripe Rule.** Take colors appear only where a take is identified, and always next to its number.

## Typography

**Display font:** Fraunces, a storybook serif with the soft and wonky axes on (`SOFT` 50–100, `WONK` 1). Fallback: Noto Serif.
**UI font:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (weights 400/600/700/800). Fallback: Atkinson Hyperlegible 400/700, mapping 600→700 and 800→700, then Cantarell.
**Mono font:** JetBrains Mono (500/600) with tabular figures. Fallback: DejaVu Sans Mono.
All three are OFL, bundled with the app, and available as Google Fonts in pen.dev.

**Character.** Atkinson's open, unambiguous letterforms (l/I/1, 0/O) carry a dense editor at 13–14 px. JetBrains Mono makes timecodes, frame numbers and file paths line up and hold still while they count. Fraunces appears in exactly three places (the wordmark, empty states, modal titles), so the storybook voice reads as a moment, not as UI chrome.

## Layout

The window is a fixed-region editor, not a page. The full geometry is in `design/BRIEF.md` §2. The default 1600×1000 window divides into:

| Region | Rect (x, y, w, h) | Ground |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Header bar | 0, 0, 1600, 48 | `surface` |

- **Spacing** follows a 4 px base: 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Inside a control use 4–8. Between controls in a group use 8. Panel padding and between groups use 16. Between sections use 24. Empty states and modals use 32–48.
- **Control sizes.** Small 24 (toolbar toggles, M/S, row icon buttons), medium 32 (default buttons, fields, segmented), large 40 (modal footers, empty-state actions).
- **Row heights.** Compact 32, default 40, layer row 44, audio track 92, bin row 72, palette row 40.
- **Density.** Dense where the eye scans (timeline, panel, palette) and generous where a decision happens (modals and empty states, with 24–48 px around content).
- **Adaptive.** niri tiles windows into columns, so widths vary. At ≥ 1400 px the panel is docked at 384 px. From 1100 to 1399 px it is docked at 320 px and the inspector fields stack 2-up. Below 1100 px the panel becomes an overlay sheet toggled from the header (layers icon). Below 820 px of window height, the timeline keeps a 232 px minimum (toolbar 36, ruler 28, video 72, two 48 px lanes) and the stage shrinks. The preview always fits its stage at the canvas aspect.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from **tonal layering**: `bg` (deepest) → `surface` (working chrome) → `raised` (things on top), with 1 px `border` hairlines between regions. Resting surfaces never carry a shadow. Shadows appear only on elements that float above the work, and they always have an offset and a soft blur (no zero-offset halos).

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **e1 Lifted** (`0 2px 6px` `shadow` at 40%, Swamp Day 16%): tooltips, a clip or layer row being dragged.
- **e2 Floating** (`0 8px 24px` `shadow` at 55% plus `0 1px 2px` at 35%; Swamp Day 20% and 12%): popovers, menus, toasts, the palette, the export panel.
- **e3 Modal** (`0 20px 48px` `shadow` at 65%; Swamp Day 25%): modals, settings, the cheat sheet sheet. Each sits on `scrim` or `scrim-strong`.

Every floating layer also gets a 1 px `border` stroke, so it separates from same-tone surfaces in dark themes.

The shadow alphas are tokens, so the GTK CSS export carries them: `shadow-e1`, `shadow-e2` plus `shadow-e2-contact`, `shadow-e3`, and `record-glow` for the REC torch (values in Colors → Derived tokens).

### Named Rules

**The Flat-at-Rest Rule.** If it doesn't float, it doesn't cast a shadow. Lift is a state (dragging, open), never a style.

## Shapes

- **Radius scale:**
  - `xs` 2: clips, meter segments, stripes.
  - `sm` 4: key caps, status chips, take-label plates.
  - `md` 6: buttons, fields, list rows, segmented controls, tooltips.
  - `lg` 12: popovers, toasts, the palette, theme cards.
  - `xl` 16: modals, settings, the cheat sheet.
  - `pill`: only the LIVE/PROGRAM/REC/PROXY badges and switch tracks.
- **Strokes** are 1 px and inner-aligned. Selection uses a 2 px inner `accent` stroke on clips, and 1 px on rows. The focus ring is 2 px `focus-ring`, 2 px outside, with the element's radius + 2.
- **No gradients, glass or glows.** The single exception is the REC torch glow, a soft `record` shadow that flickers with the badge.
- **Hatch** is the one pattern: 45° stripes, 2 px stripe and 4 px gap (6 px pitch), in `hatch-stripe`, edged with a 1 px `warning` line. In pen.dev, build it once as a reusable clipped frame of rotated rectangles.
- **Silhouettes.** The onion-with-trumpets icon is the only figurative shape in the chrome. Clips, rows and panels are plain rectangles.

## Components

Each component lists its states. Every interactive one has default, hover, pressed, focus-visible and disabled (opacity 0.45). Loading, error and empty states are listed where they exist. Sizes are logical px.

### Buttons

- **Shape and size:** `md` radius (6). Medium is 32 px tall with 0 12 padding; large is 40 px with 0 16. Label is `body-strong`. A 16 px icon sits 8 px before the label.
- **Primary** (`accent` fill, `text-on-accent`): one per region at most (Export in the export panel, Apply autocut, Add sources, Check again).
- **Record** (`record` fill, `text-on-record`; header only). It is a fixed 120×32 at (1240, 8) in every state, with its content centered, so it never moves or resizes:
  - Idle: `● Record [R]`.
  - Countdown: `Cancel [R]`, outlined (no fill, 1 px `record` stroke, `record-text` label).
  - Recording: `■ Stop [R]`.
  - Finalizing: `Saving…` with a spinner, disabled.
- **Destructive** (`record` fill, large): only in confirmations. The label is a specific verb: "Purge 2 takes", "Replace file", "Clear history", "Change fps".

### Key-cap chip

The signature of discoverability. It appears wherever a key exists.

- **Medium** (hint bar, buttons, palette, menus): 20 px tall, min-width 20, padding 0 5, `sm` radius. Fill `keycap`, 1 px `border` stroke with a **2 px bottom edge** (a pressed-key cue), legend `text` in `keycap` type.
- **Small** (tooltips): 16 tall, padding 0 4, same type.
- **Large** (cheat sheet, empty state "Press [R]"): 28 tall, min-width 28, padding 0 8, `md` radius, `keycap-lg` type.
- **Legends** follow GNOME style. Letters are uppercase as on the physical key (`R`, `S`, `C`). Symbols are literal (`[` `]` `,` `.` `<` `>` `=` `−` `\` `?`). Named keys are spelled out (`Esc`, `Tab`, `Space`, `Enter`, `Del`, `Home`, `End`), as are modifiers (`Ctrl`, `Shift`, `Alt`). Arrows are glyphs (`←` `→` `↑` `↓`). A combo is separate caps 2 px apart, with no "+".
- **Variants:**
  - **On-fill** (inside Record or primary buttons): no fill, 1 px stroke in the label color at 45%, legend in the label color.
  - **Disabled:** opacity 0.45.
  - **Rebound:** the current key, followed by `caption` `muted` "default P".

  - **Conflict:** a 1 px `warning-text` stroke plus a 12 px warning icon.

### Lists, popovers, toasts, modals

- **List row:** 32, 40, 44 or 72 tall, padding 0 12, with a leading 16 px icon or a thumbnail and trailing meta in `mono-sm` `muted`. States are hover, selected (R1), focus (ring inset), and disabled.
- **Popover:** `raised`, `lg` radius, e2, 1 px `border`, 16 px padding, with an optional 12×6 arrow. Used for the export panel, menus and Add layer.
- **Toast:** bottom-center, 12 px above the hint bar, 360–560 px wide.
  - **Build:** `raised`, `lg` radius, e2. A 20 px icon, a `body-strong` title with a `body-sm` `muted` body (up to 2 lines), up to two flat actions in `accent-text`, and a 24 px close button.
  - **Levels:** info (`info` icon, `text`), success (check, `success`), warning (triangle, `warning-text`), error (circle-x, `record-text`).
  - **Timing:** one toast at a time, queued. Info and success stay 5 s, or 8 s with actions; warning stays 8 s; error stays until dismissed.
- **Modal:** 520 wide, `raised`, `xl` radius, e3, 24 padding, over `scrim`.
  - **Content:** a `display-sm` title; a lead sentence in `body-strong`; details in `body`; an optional loss list (a `field` box, `md` radius, `mono-sm` rows with right-aligned sizes, max 8 rows visible, then scroll).
  - **Footer:** right-aligned, large buttons. **Cancel** (secondary) comes first and is **focused**; the destructive button is last.

### Inputs

- **Field and spin field:** 32 px, `field` fill, 1 px `border-strong`, `md` radius, `mono` values. Placeholders are `muted`.
- **Slider:** a 4 px track (`border-strong` unfilled, `accent` filled) with a 16 px `text` thumb that has a 2 px `bg` ring. A value field sits beside it. Ticks are 1 px `muted`. While dragging, a value tooltip shows above the thumb.
- **Toggle (switch):** a 40×22 pill.
  - **Off:** `raised` track, 1 px `border-strong`, 18 px knob in `muted`.
  - **On:** `accent` track with a `text-on-accent` knob.
- **Segmented:** the mode switch anatomy at 32 px.
- **Dropdown:** a field with a `chevron-down`.
- **Search field:** 40 px (palette) or 32 px elsewhere, with a leading `search` icon.

### Progress (the mud bar)

- **Build:** 20 px tall, `md` radius, a `field` track with a 1 px `border`. The fill is `mud`, with a 3 px `wood` sediment band along its bottom. The leading edge is rounded 6 px and wobbles ±1.5 px (2.4 s sine).
- **Bubbles:** 3–6 px circles of `mud-bubble`, at most 3 at a time. A new bubble spawns every 0.6–1.4 s, rises 6–10 px over 1.4–2.2 s (ease-in), and pops with a 120 ms fade at the surface.
- **Level easing:** the fill eases to each new value over 400 ms (ease-out).
- **Readouts:** the percentage sits outside the bar in `mono-xl`.
- **Reduced motion:** a flat `mud` fill with no bubbles or wobble; the level jumps.
- **States:** running, done (full, with a check), failed (the fill stops; the label reads "Failed at 37%" in `record-text`).
- **Indeterminate** (finalizing, analysis): 45° stripes moving 1.2 s per cycle. Under reduced motion they are static stripes with a spinner.

- **Symbolic** (16 px, one color): the bulb is a filled circle at ≥ 10 px, so at a glance it reads as ●, with the sprout and ears as 1.5 px strokes. This glyph is the REC badge motif and the idle Record button icon.
- **In pen.dev:** generate both with `Generate` type `svg`; never draw them from hand paths. The empty-state illustrations (a quiet swamp pool with the onion, and a wooden signpost) are generated the same way.

### Motion system

| Moment | Duration | Easing | Reduced motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hover, press, toggle | 120 ms | `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` | same (color only) |
| State change (selection, segment, row) | 180 ms | `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` | 120 ms opacity/color only |
| Popover, menu, palette in / out | 200 / 140 ms | `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` / `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)` | fade 120 ms, no scale |
| Bin drawer and panel overlay | 240 / 160 ms | expo-out / ease-in | crossfade 120 ms |
| Toast in / out | 200 / 140 ms | expo-out (12 px rise) / ease-in fade | fade only |
| Timeline zoom (`=`, `−`, Ctrl+wheel, `\`, `z`) | 200 ms at 60 fps, anchored at the cursor or playhead | `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` | instant |
| Drag-move neighbors closing the gap | 120 ms | expo-out | instant |
| REC torch flicker | 1600 ms loop | stepped keyframes (see REC badge) | static |
| Countdown digit / ring | 160 ms (scale 1.12 → 1, fade) / 1000 ms linear sweep | expo-out / linear | swap / static ring |
| Mud level / bubbles / wobble | 400 ms / 1.4–2.2 s / 2.4 s loop | ease-out / ease-in / sine | jump / none / none |
| Playhead during playback | per frame, no easing; auto-page jumps a page | none | same |
| Live meters | real-time, 1.5 s peak-hold decay | none | same (they convey state) |

Nothing animates on app start. Spinners keep spinning under reduced motion, because they are essential feedback. The optional "squelch" sound (off by default) plays on record start and stop only.

### Copy on screen

- **Labels are plain verbs:** Record, Stop, Export…, Save frame, Split clip, Autocut, Insert at end, Purge from disk, Open file, Open folder, Copy path, Check again. **Swamp flavor lives only in** secondary text, toasts, empty states and modal titles.
- **Pinned strings (verbatim):**
  - "This swamp is quiet. Press **R** and make some noise."
  - "Get out of my swamp?"
  - "Layers! Like an onion. Your video is ready."
  - "That was shorter than Lord Farquaad. Take moved to the bin."
  - "Recovered take 7 from the bog."
  - "The swamp is missing something"
  - "Not my swamp: this folder isn't empty and has no qrec.json"
- **Errors** name what failed, where (which source, which clip) and the recovery. They never joke ("Screen access to DP-4 was denied", not a swamp pun).
- **Destructive dialogs** open with a plain lead sentence stating the action and the loss, with exact counts and sizes. The flavored title comes first, the facts second.
- **Units and formats:** timecode `HH:MM:SS:FF`; sizes in `MB` and `GB` with one decimal; gain in dB with one decimal (`−6.0 dB`); offsets in ms with a sign (`+80 ms`); the true minus sign (−) in values.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** keep every timecode, frame count, size, dB and ms value in JetBrains Mono (`mono`, `mono-sm`, `mono-xs`).
- **Do** show the key next to every action that has one: a key cap in the hint bar and on the Record button, and the key in tooltips, menus, the palette (with the CLI form) and the cheat sheet.
- **Do** use `record-text`, `accent-text` and `warning-text` for any colored text or icon ≤ 20 px on `bg`, `surface` or `raised`.
- **Do** turn all text on a selected row to `text`, and mark the selection with a 1 px `accent` inner stroke (2 px on clips).
- **Do** focus **Cancel** first in every "Get out of my swamp?" modal, and make the destructive label a specific verb with a count ("Purge 2 takes").
- **Do** give every animation its reduced-motion fallback from the motion table, and keep meters, spinners and progress live.
- **Do** put take colors only on take stripes, audio segment tints and bin thumbnails, always beside the take number.
- **Do** build the onion icon, the REC glyph and illustrations with pen.dev `Generate` (svg), and keep the app chrome free of figurative ornament.

### Don't:

- **Don't** use `record` for anything except recording and destructive actions. No red errors-as-decoration, no red badges, and nothing else pulses.
- **Don't** set Fraunces on buttons, labels, numbers, menus or the hint bar.
- **Don't** put `accent-2` (onion gold) in text.
- **Don't** use grey. Every neutral comes from its theme's tinted tokens.
- **Don't** add shadows to resting surfaces, gradients to fills, glass, glows (except the REC torch glow) or colored side-stripes over 1 px on rows or cards.
- **Don't** put jokes in button labels, menu items or error recovery steps.
- **Don't** show REC state by color alone. It always carries the ● onion glyph, the word REC and the flicker (or the static badge under reduced motion).
- **Don't** shrink a clip below its true duration width to fit a label. Narrow clips lose the label first, then the number, and keep the 1 px edge marker.
