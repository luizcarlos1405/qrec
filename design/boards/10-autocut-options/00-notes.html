Autocut options

Issue #15: three options for autocut (SPEC §9) on the autocut page (S12) and Settings › Autocut. Not approved yet (the options' boards are, the analyzing cue is up for review). The boards are S12 with only what changes; everything else on the page is as built.

What's on the board

  1. S12 · Keep a previewed cut: every region row gets a check box (checked = cut). Silence 2 was clicked on the timeline (or unchecked in the list): it draws as kept, the summary says Removes 3 silences (4.8 s) · 1 kept, and the Take and Timeline lines count it back. The pointer is on silence 3, with its tooltip.
  2. S12 · Trim edges only: the new check box under the sliders, on. Only the silence before the first sound and after the last is hatched (2.2 s and 0.9 s); the four pauses inside stay. The summary says Trims 2 edges (3.1 s).
  3. S12 · Two tracks · dropdown: Detect silence on stays a dropdown; its menu has a check box per track in the scope. The value reads A1 · Yeti Nano + A2 · System audio. Silence 3 is no longer cut, because System audio plays a sound there. Both lanes are hatched, the tag shows once, and A2's header gets the scissors.
  4. S12 · Two tracks · inline: the same, but the tracks are listed as check boxes on the page. It's one click less, but it takes a row per track.
  5. S12 · Analyzing: the threshold just moved to −40 dB, so take 8 is being analyzed again. The summary title becomes Analyzing take 8 · 40% with a progress bar under it. The old plan's numbers and regions dim (the panel keeps its layout). On the timeline the old hatches are gone, and the take plate spins, as it does for a real autocut job.
  6. Span states at 48 px/s: cut, cut with the pointer on it, kept, kept with the pointer on it. Also the region rows: selected, kept, and a box with keyboard focus.
  7. Settings › Autocut: a Trim edges only switch for new projects. Other settings in the dialog are saved at once, so this one is a switch here and a check box (a draft until Apply) on S12.

Decided (2026-11-15)

  • Exceptions survive a slider change. The core first plans without them, marks every span that overlaps a keep range as kept (the whole span), then plans again with those spans as sound. A kept range that no longer overlaps any span is dropped. The GUI sends ranges that are exactly a span; the CLI's --keep <clip>:<in>-<out> takes any range.
  • One threshold for all chosen tracks. A threshold per track waits for issue #1 (detecting the threshold).
  • Intersected silences are checked against Min silence again, so a 100 ms overlap of two long silences isn't cut.
  • The track picker is the dropdown (board 3). Settings › Autocut gets no track choice: a new project listens to its first mic. No extra key for keeping a span: the region list's boxes are the keyboard path. The wording stays as drawn.
  • Several tracks: keep only the intersection of their silences (sound on any chosen track keeps the region), never the union.
  • Exceptions are part of the page's draft: Cancel or Esc drops them, and "Run autocut after every take" never uses them. Edges only and the tracks do apply after every take.

The analyzing cue (decided 2026-11-15)

  • A percentage. The analysis reports ffmpeg's position (-progress) as it reads each audio file. Position ÷ the file's duration, with each file weighted by its length and cached analyses left out, gives one figure for the whole run (Analyzing 3 takes · 40%).
  • No flicker: the cue shows only when the new plan takes longer than 300 ms. Re-plans (Padding, Min keep, edges only, exceptions) and cached analyses never show it.
  • The old plan is hidden on the timeline while it's being replaced. On the page its numbers and regions dim instead, so the panel doesn't reflow.
